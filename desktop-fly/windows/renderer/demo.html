    WHAT YOU ARE WATCHING

    Real wiring. A simplified simulation.

    Researchers reconstructed neurons and their connections from electron microscopy. This app loads selected FlyWire brain circuits and a MaleCNS nerve-cord circuit.

    1. Input: buttons inject current into named neurons. Loom injects a synthetic expanding-object signal into LC4/LPLC2.
    2. Neurons: leaky integrate-and-fire equations run in 1 ms steps. Measured connection counts and transmitter annotations inform modeled synaptic weights.
    3. Movement: descending activity crosses a modeled interface into the nerve cord. Motor-neuron rates produce 36 muscle commands. Joint motion and ground contact move the body; joint feedback returns to sensory neurons.
    4. Readout: rates and Giant Fiber events come from the simulation. Forward/backward labels come from body displacement. They are observations, not mind reading.

    Demo choices: the tour schedules stimulation, not poses. This walking assay suppresses grooming, heuristic nervous darts and spontaneous flights. Giant Fiber escape remains enabled. Its flight trajectory is procedural. Neuron parameters, body forces and the female-to-male interface are assumptions; nothing learns here.

    Whole brain? FlyWire contains about 139,000 neurons. This app simulates 668 of them plus 1,045 nerve-cord neurons from a different fly. Whole-brain simulation needs a larger runtime, complete graph data and validated sensory/motor interfaces; loading a point cloud does not accomplish that.
